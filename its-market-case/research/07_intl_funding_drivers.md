## 1. Key takeaways

1. **ERTMS/ETCS is the single largest regulation-driven ITS demand pool outside the US, and it is behind schedule.** The European Commission's Third ERTMS Work Plan (Feb 2026) estimates **EUR 75-85 bn** for trackside ERTMS on the whole TEN-T network (EUR 37-42 bn on the core network) plus **EUR 10-15 bn** for on-board fitment (EUR 5-10 bn for core-network vehicles), i.e. **EUR 85-100 bn** in total. By end-2024 only **~10% of TEN-T (12,400 km)** had ETCS and only **~19% of the EU fleet (8,730 vehicles)** was equipped; the Commission says deployment is "significantly behind schedule" against the TEN-T deadlines (core 2030, extended core 2040, comprehensive 2050). The gap between mandate and delivery is the demand backlog for signalling vendors through 2031 and beyond.
2. **EU co-funding is material but only covers a fraction:** CEF Transport 2021-27 is **EUR 25.8 bn** (EUR 11.2 bn cohesion envelope, EUR 1.75 bn military mobility). CEF 2 has so far funded 48 ERTMS projects (**EUR 638 m trackside for ~3,600 km of ETCS; EUR 414 m on-board for ~5,200 vehicles**), implying average CEF unit support of roughly EUR 0.18 m/km and EUR 0.08 m/vehicle. The 2026 CEF Transport call offers EUR 1.1 bn against EUR ~3 bn requested (246 applications). The RRF adds about EUR 38.4 bn for rail (40% of transport-related RRP spend), with ERTMS explicitly in the Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Bulgarian, Finnish and Romanian plans. Member-state balance sheets (DB InfraGO, Network Rail, RFI, Adif, ProRail) carry the rest.
3. **Germany is the swing market.** The federal GVFG (local transit capital grants) is fixed in law at **EUR 2.0 bn/yr from 2025, indexed +1.8%/yr from 2026** (up from EUR 1.0 bn in 2021-24); the Deutschlandticket is funded at **EUR 1.5 bn/yr federal + EUR 1.5 bn/yr Länder** (federal share now committed to 2030; ~14.5-14.6 m subscribers); and Digitale Schiene Deutschland signed a **EUR 6.3 bn volume contract (Mar 2025)** with Siemens Mobility/Leonhard Weiss, Alstom, Hitachi Rail and MerMec for digital interlockings (DSTW), ETCS and integrated control systems, with construction into the 2030s. Germany still had only 3% of its 2030 core-corridor scope in ETCS operation (2023 data) and set up a national ERTMS Coordination Office in April 2026 to fund on-board fitment. Rail infrastructure investment need per DB InfraGO's 2025 condition report: EUR 124 bn.
4. **UK: a funded five-year rail settlement plus a bus reform/funding reset.** ORR's PR23 final determination funds Network Rail at **GBP 43.1 bn for CP7 (Apr 2024-Mar 2029)**; Network Rail's July 2026 plan shows GBP 45.1 bn total funding, GBP 18.7 bn renewals and other capital, GBP 13.7 bn maintenance. The digital-signalling portfolio in CP7 is **GBP 1.54 bn** (GBP 927 m allocated to the Industry Partnership Digital Rail, incl. GBP 0.5 bn fleet fitment, GBP 0.2 bn ETCS signalling renewals, GBP 0.2 bn enablers); the East Coast Digital Programme (>GBP 1 bn) is live (Northern City Line signal-free since 2025; Welwyn-Hitchin ETCS overlay commissioned, Class 717 in use from Aug 2026, more fleets from 2027, completion in the 2030s). Buses: Spending Review 2025 gives **GBP 15.6 bn Transport for City Regions to 2031-32**, **>GBP 1 bn/yr** bus funding in England outside London (LABG: GBP 481 m revenue/yr + ~GBP 200 m capital/yr to 2029-30), a GBP 2 fare cap in 2027 (GBP 400 m) and the **Bus Services Act 2025** (franchising powers). ZEBRA 1+2 funded roughly **GBP 420 m** of zero-emission buses (ZEBRA 2: up to 955 buses, GBP 142.8 m). Great British Railways: Railways Bill introduced 5 Nov 2025; GBR operational ~12 months after Royal Assent.
5. **France: Grand Paris Express is the largest urban CBTC build in Europe** (200 km, 68 stations, four new automated lines + Line 14 extension, opening in stages 2024-2030, EUR 39 bn authorised debt ceiling). Suburban rail resignalling is next: Alstom's **~EUR 300 m NExTEO (CBTC) framework for RER B and D** (Nov 2023) and the EUR 230 m Line 18 automation/rolling-stock contract show the ticket sizes. The SERM programme (regional express networks) is a second wave of regional demand.
6. **Italy/Spain/Netherlands: RRF-financed ERTMS at national scale.** RFI's framework agreements (Alstom lot: ~EUR 900 m for ~1,400 km / 27 lines of ETCS L2 B3 + GSM-R + digital interlockings) are the European benchmark for ERTMS unit economics (~EUR 0.64 m/km incl. interlockings). Spain's Adif (11,676 km network) draws EUR 87.4 m CEF and EUR 146.9 m ERDF in 2021-27 plus RRF lines for Cercanías and TEN-T corridors. The Netherlands is replacing ATB with ERTMS in tranches; tranche 1 covers **419 km** (Northern lines, Zeeland line, Kijfhoek-Belgian border).
7. **India: Kavach is a domestic ATP mandate with explicit volumes and slipping dates.** Allocation **INR 1,112.57 crore in FY2024-25** (INR 1,216.77 crore spent to date, Jul 2024); 1,465 route-km and 144 locomotives commissioned by mid-2024; sanction for **10,000 locomotives**; target **5,000 route-km per year**; ~3,000 route-km Delhi-Mumbai/Delhi-Howrah slipped from Mar 2025 to Dec 2025 to 2026 (~25% commissioned). Unit cost from recent sanctions: **INR 252 crore for 607.7 route-km** of Kavach 4.0 (~INR 0.41 crore/km, about USD 50k per route-km trackside). Only three approved Kavach vendors (Medha, HBL Power, Kernex) as of 2024. Metro: India had **881 km** of metro at end-2023 (UITP), and e-bus procurement is being aggregated by CESL (10,900 e-buses tendered Jun 2025 under PM E-DRIVE; 6,230 more in Jan 2026) under 12-year gross-cost contracts that bundle telematics, passenger information and depot charging.
8. **China is the largest urban-rail installed base but is now decelerating.** CAMET: **12,160.77 km** in operation at end-2024 (361 lines; +936 km in 2024), **5,833 km under construction** in 44 cities, but completed construction investment fell **8.9% YoY to RMB 474.9 bn** in 2024 (third successive fall), rolling-stock purchases fell 12.4%, and the approved-but-unbuilt planning pipeline (5,532 km, RMB 3,971 bn) declined 2.8% YoY. Growth is shifting from new lines to smart-operations retrofits (CAMET "Smart Urban Rail Outline V2.0, 2026-2035"). Domestic CBTC suppliers dominate.
9. **Global urban-rail pipeline (UITP Global Metro Figures 2024):** 20,453 km of metro in 202 cities at end-2023 (+18% vs 2020), 831 lines, 158,871 cars, 58 bn annual riders; ~800 km/yr of new metro opened on average 2010-23 (84% in Asia-Pacific); automated (GoA4) lines reached 2,279 km (11% of total, +75% 2020-23, 60 cities). Asia-Pacific holds 63% of km; Europe 14%; North America 8%.
10. **Middle East, Australia, Canada, LatAm add greenfield and policy-driven demand:** Riyadh Metro (176 km, 6 automated lines, 85 stations, 190 trains; 842-bus network incl. 160 km of BRT lanes; 470 m+ metro+bus riders to Q3 2026); Sydney Metro (113 km, 46 stations, 4 lines; Metro West opening 2032; Western Sydney Airport line PPP) plus Alstom's 20-year ETCS L2 framework for Sydney Trains and the ETCS contract within Brisbane's Cross River Rail (services by 2029); Canada's **Permanent Public Transit Fund of C$3 bn/yr from 2026-27** (Budget 2021; Budget 2024 ties access to transit-oriented zoning); Chile's EFE EUR 55 m network-wide signalling modernisation (1,800 km, control centre + onboard).
11. **Bus electrification is now a procurement rule, not a pilot, in the EU and UK:** the Clean Vehicles Directive requires 45% clean buses (half zero-emission) in 2021-25 rising to **65% in 2026-30** for DE/IT/NL/SE/BE/AT and most western Member States (FR 43%/61%, ES 45%/65%, PL 32%/46%); ACEA data cited by Sustainable Bus show electrically chargeable buses/coaches at **26.4% of EU registrations in Jan-Sep 2026 (8,633 units, +37.6% YoY)**. Each new ZEB carries new onboard telematics/APC/CCTV/PIS and depot-charging software, so ZEB fleet turnover is a direct ITS-hardware refresh driver.
12. **Risks for the forecast:** ERTMS slippage and fragmentation (EC Oct-2026 study: project-specific national requirements and vendor lock-in raise trackside costs); public-budget stress (UK CP7 had a ~GBP 1 bn funding gap, Network Rail has scaled back some renewals; China investment contraction; Germany's infrastructure backlog); deadline extensions (Kavach, ECDP "into the 2030s"); and the fact that EU grants fund only a small share of unit cost.

## 3. Programme notes by geography (what the numbers mean for ITS demand)

### 3.1 European Union - CEF, TEN-T and ERTMS
- **Mandate:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1679 (applies since 18 Jul 2024) sets completion of the core network by 2030, extended core by 2040, comprehensive by 2050, and ERTMS across the whole TEN-T with national Class B systems phased out. The Commission's ERTMS pages still describe the older 2030 core / 2050 comprehensive obligation and note the European Deployment Plan is being re-cut for 2024-2040.
- **Cost envelope:** EUR 75-85 bn trackside + EUR 10-15 bn on-board (Third Work Plan, Feb 2026). Against this, EU grants are small: CEF 2 has committed about EUR 1.05 bn to ERTMS so far (EUR 638 m trackside, EUR 414 m on-board), Cohesion/ERDF a further ~EUR 555 m, and the RRF funds national programmes (IT, ES, NL, RO, BG, FI).
- **Status:** ~10% of TEN-T (12,400 km) and ~19% of the fleet (8,730 vehicles) at end-2024. In 2023 the Commission counted 21,744 vehicles to be equipped by 2030 (7,539 done). Only Luxembourg, the Netherlands (planned sections) and Switzerland were essentially complete; France, Germany, Italy, Poland and Spain had the most km still to do.
- **Pipeline signals:** 2026 CEF Transport call EUR 1.1 bn vs ~EUR 3 bn requested; 45 industry bodies asking for a >=EUR 100 bn CEF post-2027; Commission study (Oct 2026) blames fragmentation and vendor lock-in for high trackside costs and recommends Class-B-free deployment and standard products. For a new entrant this is both the barrier (country-specific specs, incumbent lock-in) and the opening (push for standardised, industrialised products and simplified authorisation).
- **Bus:** Clean Vehicles Directive second period (2026-2030) raises clean-bus procurement minimums to 65% in most western Member States (half zero-emission); ACEA-based data show electrically chargeable buses at 26.4% of EU registrations in Jan-Sep 2026.

### 3.2 Germany
- GVFG federal capital grants doubled to EUR 2 bn in 2025 with 1.8% indexation; Deutschlandticket EUR 3 bn/yr public support (federal share committed to 2030, 14.5-14.6 m subscribers); Regionalisierungsmittel base EUR 8.2 bn/yr (+1.8%/yr to 2031).
- Digitale Schiene Deutschland: EUR 6.3 bn industry volume contract (Mar 2025) for DSTW/ETCS/iLBS with Siemens Mobility + Leonhard Weiss, Alstom, Hitachi Rail and MerMec; starter package (Cologne-Rhine/Main 173 km ETCS / 300 km DSTW; Stuttgart Digital Node incl. ATO GoA2; ScanMed corridor), Rhine-Alpine corridor ~1,362 km of ETCS by 2040, Flensburg-Maschen 249 km, Berlin-Halle/Leipzig 185 km, Nuremberg-Passau ~220 km. First commissionings: ~60 km of ETCS on German-Swiss border sections (Dec 2025), Rhine Valley Freiburg-Basel ETCS L2 (2026), Hitachi's first German RBC (Jul 2025).
- Germany had only 3% of its 2030 core-corridor scope in ETCS operation (2023 data); BMV admitted lagging and created an ERTMS Coordination Office (Apr 2026) that will fund on-board fitment. Rail investment need EUR 124 bn (InfraGO 2025). Budget-cut specifics for DSD were not found on reachable sources (see gaps).

### 3.3 United Kingdom
- Rail: ORR PR23 GBP 43.1 bn CP7 settlement; Network Rail's Year-3 plan GBP 45.1 bn total, GBP 18.7 bn renewals/other capital; digital signalling portfolio GBP 1.54 bn (vs GBP 0.99 bn in 2024 plan) including GBP 0.5 bn fleet fitment and GBP 0.2 bn ETCS signalling renewals; ECDP >GBP 1 bn with first ETCS-only operation on the Northern City Line (2025), Welwyn-Hitchin overlay in use (Aug 2026), completion in the 2030s; LTDP scale >4,000 trains / >19,000 miles. CP7 had a ~GBP 1 bn funding gap (now closed in England & Wales), and the plan says some renewals were scaled back - a demand-timing risk. GBR: Railways Bill introduced 5 Nov 2025; GBR operational ~12 months after Royal Assent.
- Bus: SR2025 - GBP 15.6 bn Transport for City Regions to 2031-32 (explicitly for zero-emission buses, trams, local rail), >GBP 1 bn/yr LABG (GBP 481 m revenue + ~GBP 200 m capital per year), GBP 2 fare cap in 2027 (GBP 400 m), "more than GBP 3 bn for buses over the next 3 years", Bus Services Act 2025 (franchising, LTA data powers). ZEBRA 1 (~GBP 277 m) and ZEBRA 2 (GBP 142.8 m, up to 955 buses) seeded ZEB fleets; TCR settlements are now the main ZEB funding channel.

### 3.4 France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands
- France: GPE 200 km / 68 stations / EUR 39 bn debt ceiling, opening 2024-2030 (Alstom Line 18 EUR 230 m CBTC + trains); NExTEO CBTC on RER B/D ~EUR 300 m; SERM regional express networks starting 2026.
- Italy: RFI ERTMS frameworks (Alstom South-Centre lot ~EUR 900 m, ~1,400 km, 27 lines, ETCS L2 B3 + GSM-R + ACCM interlockings); Italy's RRP includes ERTMS; 11% of CNC scope in operation (2023).
- Spain: Adif 11,676 km; CEF EUR 87.4 m and ERDF EUR 146.9 m (2021-27); RRF lines for Cercanías and TEN-T corridors; ERTMS for low-density lines R&D (EUR 13.7 m); 22% of CNC scope in operation (2023).
- Netherlands: ERTMS replaces ATB nationwide in tranches; tranche 1 = 419 km (Northern lines, Zeeland line, Kijfhoek-Belgian border) with a new central safety system, IT changes and fleet retrofit/upgrades; new programme plan in preparation. Dutch sections planned to 2023 were already complete (EC).

### 3.5 India
- Kavach (ATP): INR 1,112.57 crore FY25 budget; 1,465 RKM + 144 locos by mid-2024; sanction for 10,000 locomotives; 5,000 RKM/yr target; 3,000 RKM high-density routes slipped to 2026 (~25% done Dec 2025); recent sanctions imply ~INR 0.41 crore/RKM (Kavach 4.0, Lucknow 607.7 km for INR 252 crore). Vendor base of three (Medha, HBL, Kernex) with more approvals expected - an unusually concentrated and protected ATP market.
- Metro: 881 km at end-2023 (UITP); city networks still expanding (Bengaluru 103.6 km; Pune Phase II INR 13,500 crore). L&T winning Dhaka Metro Line 1 systems (INR 5,000-10,000 crore) shows Indian integrators exporting.
- Buses: CESL aggregation (10,900 e-buses Jun 2025; 6,230 Jan 2026; 220 Gujarat under PM-eBus Sewa) on 12-year gross-cost contracts bundling ITS/telematics and charging; private operators scaling (GreenCell >5,000 buses, 105 cities).

### 3.6 China
- 12,161 km in operation (end-2024), 5,833 km under construction, but construction investment down 8.9% in 2024 (RMB 474.9 bn), rolling-stock purchases down 12.4%, approved-plan pipeline down 2.8% - a clear deceleration from the 2010s. CAMET's Smart Urban Rail outline (2026-2035) redirects spend to digital operations (smart stations, AI, maintenance). Domestic CBTC and systems vendors dominate; foreign ITS vendors have limited access.

### 3.7 Australia, Middle East, Latin America, Canada, Japan
- Australia: Sydney Metro 113 km / 46 stations (Metro West 2032; WSA PPP with 12 trains and core systems); Sydney Trains ETCS L2 20-year framework; Cross River Rail (ETCS, services 2029). Melbourne/Victoria figures not captured.
- Middle East: Riyadh Metro 176 km / 6 GoA4 lines / 190 trains, 842 buses incl. 160 km BRT lanes, 470 m+ riders by Q3 2026; AlUla tram >EUR 500 m. Dubai Blue Line not sourced.
- Latin America: 1,080 km of metro; EFE Chile network signalling EUR 55 m (1,800 km). BRT/e-bus counts not captured.
- Canada: C$3 bn/yr Permanent Public Transit Fund from 2026-27, conditional on transit-oriented zoning (Budget 2024); CIB >C$11 bn across 50+ projects.
- Japan: 973 km of metro (UITP); no funding programme data captured.
