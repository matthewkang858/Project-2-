# 36 - Gap fill: Non-US rail resignalling, ETCS and CBTC pipeline 2026-2031 (country by country)

**Topic:** Quantified, committed-versus-planned pipeline of mainline ERTMS/ETCS resignalling and urban CBTC resignalling outside the US, consolidated into km, vehicles, EUR value and timing by country, with a 2026-31 annual phasing (base/low/high) and explicit slippage flags. Builds on files 07 (funding drivers), 12 (unit costs) and 14 (operator capital plans); fills the national-budget gaps those files left open (NL, DK, NO, CH, BE, AT, IT PNRR, ES, DSD).
**Prepared:** 2026-11-07. Currency as stated in source; EUR conversions use the analyst FX assumptions stated in section 6 (not sourced).
**Data files:** `data/rail_resignalling_pipeline.csv` (49 rows: 25 EU Member States + EU total, 6 non-EU mainline rows, 17 urban rows) and `data/rail_resignalling_pipeline_phasing_2026_2031.csv` (annual phasing, base/low/high).
**Method note:** WebFetch was blocked by the session network policy (ENOTFOUND on every host); all primary sources were retrieved with `curl` through the session proxy and PDFs converted with `pdftotext`. The shared WebSearch budget (200 calls per turn across all agents) ran out after ~20 queries in this run; after that point only direct fetches of known URLs were possible, which is why a few items (Hong Kong MTR signalling line item, Paris line-by-line programme values, Poland/Czechia/France national budgets, Sydney Digital Systems total) remain analyst estimates or EC-km-based rather than nationally sourced. Every number below was read on the page or PDF cited.

## 1. Key takeaways

1. **The EU backlog is now officially quantified and officially behind schedule.** The European ERTMS Coordinator's Third Work Plan (23 Feb 2026) puts ETCS at 12,400 km (10%) of the 119,988 km TEN-T network and 10,584 km (17%) of the 63,959 km core network at end-2024, with 16,400 km more under construction. Member-State plans add ~28,000 km by 2030 (12,200 km in 2025-27 "in principle achievable", 15,700 km in 2028-30 "depends on scale-up"), i.e. ~4,650 km/yr versus ~1,000 km/yr achieved on the corridors in 2022-24. Remaining cost: EUR 75-85 bn trackside (EUR 37-42 bn core) plus EUR 10-15 bn on-board (13,400 retrofits x EUR 0.9 m). The Commission's own unit cost is **~EUR 850k per km of line including interlockings** (15-25% design/PM, 75-85% field), which sits between the RFI framework rates (EUR 0.46-0.79 m/km) and the Stuttgart node rate (EUR 1.4 m/track-km) in file 12.
2. **Core remaining to 2030 = 21,940 km, concentrated in six countries.** Core-network km still to equip between end-2024 and the 2030 plans: Poland 3,742, Italy 2,993, Germany 2,694, Romania 2,521, Spain 1,856, Portugal 1,020, Greece 979, France 844, Czechia 790, Austria 752. The Commission classes Germany, Poland, Italy and Romania as "even more challenging" (~90% of 2030 km still to do) and Sweden, Croatia, France and Finland as "clearly short" of the TEN-T target (<25% of core by 2030).
3. **National budgets now sourced for every country flagged as a gap:** Netherlands EUR 6.1 bn needed to 2035 (EUR 3.0 bn available + 1.5 bn reserved + **EUR 1.6 bn added 5-6 Nov 2026**, plus EUR 0.3 bn to finish tranche 1; EUR 14.4 bn to 2050 vs EUR 11.8 bn reserved; tranche-1 budget EUR 2,880 m vs forecast EUR 4,194 m); Denmark DKK 21.7 bn (EUR 2.9 bn) for the whole network, completion 2033; Belgium EUR 2 bn (2013 prices) spent to 2025 for a now-complete 6,399 km network (~EUR 2.8 bn incl. all) plus EUR 1 bn infra 2025-32 and >= EUR 499 m on-board 2025-30; Norway NOK 25 bn programme to 2034 (Siemens NKr 5.5 bn trackside, Alstom NKr 2 bn on-board, Thales/Hitachi NKr 0.6 bn TMS, Norske tog NOK 1.1 bn); Switzerland CHF 7 bn to 2045 confirmed by BAV/SBB (29 Oct 2026) against only CHF 1 bn in the 2027 federal dispatch; Austria EUR 2.6 bn digital-interlocking frameworks (Siemens/Hitachi, 22 Sep 2026, ~300 DSTW, pilot Q4 2030) alongside ETCS L2 on the entire network by 2038; Italy PNRR EUR 2.5 bn (of a EUR 2.97 bn accelerated plan) for ~2,800 km by Jun 2026 (1,400 km done Jul 2025; target cut from 3,400 km), EUR 2.7 bn frameworks for 4,220 km, then an unfunded EUR 5 bn tranche (~EUR 700 m/yr to 2031 for ~3,700 km in 2029-33) and EUR 9 bn in 2032-40 within an EUR 18 bn total; Spain EUR 2.2 bn ERTMS infrastructure (within EUR 5.1 bn modernisation), EUR 250 m on-board to 2045, EUR 280 m Rodalies; Sweden SEK 50 bn total / SEK 22 bn to 2044; Germany EUR 6.3 bn DSD volume contract plus **EUR 1.7 bn federal on-board funding to 2030 (+EUR 482 m Digital Node Stuttgart)**.
4. **Committed vs planned:** of the ~EUR 36 bn base-case 2026-31 non-US spend, roughly 55-60% is covered by signed frameworks, volume contracts or parliamentary appropriations (DSD EUR 6.3 bn, RFI EUR 2.7 bn + PNRR, NL tranche 1, DK, BE, NO, UK CP7 GBP 994 m, Toronto L2, Madrid L6, Copenhagen, Berlin, Oslo, HK MTR, Chinese renewal awards). The rest depends on budget decisions still open: Italy's EUR 5 bn 2029-33 tranche, Switzerland's CHF 6 bn gap, Germany's LuFV InfraGO ETCS portfolio (DB must table a network-wide ERTMS proposal by end-2026), Poland/Romania CEF-dependent tenders, UK CP8 (2029-34), and London's unfunded Piccadilly ATC.
5. **Base-case phasing 2026-31 (non-US, EUR m): 4,350 / 5,350 / 6,140 / 6,760 / 7,040 / 6,140 = EUR 35.8 bn** (low EUR 20.6 bn; high EUR 54.7 bn). Composition in the base case: EU ETCS trackside EUR 17.1 bn (20,100 km at EUR 0.85 m/km), EU on-board EUR 4.2 bn (7,700 vehicles at a blended EUR 0.55 m), non-EU mainline (UK, CH, NO, IN, AU) EUR 5.8 bn, named urban CBTC programmes EUR 5.7 bn plus a EUR 3.0 bn allowance for unlisted metro/LRT resignalling. The per-country bottom-up of national budgets gives EUR 21.8 bn for EU trackside, 27% above the EC-km x unit-cost figure, which is consistent with the Commission's observation that Member-State estimates "are often considerably higher" because they include interlocking renewals, capacity works and non-TEN-T lines.
6. **Unit-cost cross-check against file 12 holds.** Mainline: EC EUR 0.85 m/km (incl. interlockings) vs RFI EUR 0.46-0.79 m/km, Norway NOK 25 bn / 4,200 km = ~EUR 0.5 m/km (incl. TMS and on-board), Switzerland CHF 7 bn / ~3,200 km = ~CHF 2.2 m/km over 20 years (incl. interlockings), Kavach USD 40-61k/km. On-board: EC EUR 0.9 m retrofit / EUR 0.4 m upgrade vs ÖBB EUR 245k upgrade, Stuttgart EUR 605-665k incl. ATO, Norway NKr 2 bn / 450 veh = ~EUR 375k, UK GBP 535 m fleet fitment in CP7. Urban: Toronto L2 C$402 m / 26 km = ~EUR 10.3 m/km (vs TTC L1 EUR 9.7 m/km), Madrid L6 signalling-only EUR 85 m / 23.5 km = EUR 3.6 m/km (plus EUR 112 m PSD), Berlin EUR 5 m/km, Copenhagen EUR 1.6 m/km, Guangzhou L3 RMB 568 m / 69 km = ~EUR 1.0 m/km and L2 RMB 439 m / 31.8 km = ~EUR 1.7 m/km (domestic replacement of Siemens/Alcatel first-generation systems).
7. **Urban resignalling is a second, smaller but faster-growing wave.** UITP: 2,279 km of GoA4 metro (11% of 20,453 km) at end-2023, +75% since 2020; 72% of automated km use CBTC; the 2018 brief projected >3,800 km by 2028. China's first CBTC generation (lines opened 2002-2012) is reaching life-expiry: ~1,400 km of urban rail is >15 years old and >2,000 km is 10-15 years old (of 13,000 km at end-2025); H1-2026 signalling awards totalled RMB 2.68 bn of which RMB 1.88 bn (70%) were renewals (Guangzhou L2/L3/APM RMB 1.07 bn, Nanjing L2 RMB 519 m, Shanghai 9/10/11/16/17 radio RMB 263 m), with Guangzhou L5/L8, Xi'an L2, Shenzhen L1 and Suzhou L1 tendering in H2-2026; CAMET expects ~RMB 49.1 bn/yr of existing-line renovation (all systems) over the next five years. Hong Kong MTR's SACEM-to-CBTC replacement enters service on the Tsuen Wan Line in mid-March 2026 with Island/Kwun Tong/Tseung Kwan O to follow and completion ~2029.
8. **Slippage is the rule, not the exception, and must be in the forecast:** EC "significantly behind schedule"; DB's ETCS migration strategy withdrawn (summer 2025) and only 20.13% of 2025 federal ETCS funds drawn, 2027 draft budget cuts EUR 1.2 bn from rail digitalisation/renewal; UK CP7 ETCS cut from GBP 1,544 m to GBP 994 m, West Coast North first ETCS commissioning deferred to mid-CP8 (2028-35), ECDP re-baselined; Netherlands tranche-1 budget strain EUR 219 m and a second strategy reset; Denmark 2030 to 2033; Belgium on-board deadline extended to Dec 2027; Italy PNRR target cut by 615 km; Kavach deadlines missed twice; Melbourne HCS scope halved (54 to 27 km); Toronto L2 completion 2037; Madrid-Sevilla LZB replacement slipped past Jan 2026; Austria's DSTW pilot not before Q4 2030; Switzerland CHF 1 bn vs CHF 7 bn need. The base case therefore assumes ~55% of planned EU km are delivered in-window; the high case equals the NIPs.

Implied CAGR 2026-30 of the base case: +12.8% p.a.; low +8.3%; high +6.6% (high case is front-loaded by plan). Read-across for the forecast: the committed share (frameworks/volume contracts/appropriations) covers roughly EUR 20-22 bn of the EUR 35.8 bn base, so the base case needs ~EUR 14 bn of decisions still to be taken (IT 2029-33 tranche, CH, DE LuFV, PL/RO tenders, UK CP8, Chinese H2 tenders) - these are the swing items for a 2031 view. Cross-check versus top-down: SCI's European CCS market (file 15/21) and the EC's EUR 17.9 bn (2023) global signalling market at 4.6% CAGR imply ~EUR 20-22 bn in 2027; the base-case non-US resignalling pipeline of EUR 5-7 bn/yr is therefore ~25-35% of the global signalling market, consistent with modernisation-led growth.
