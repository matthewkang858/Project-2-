# 05 — Global (non-US) bottom-up demand base: fleets, networks, operators

**Purpose.** Anchor the per-asset sizing of the Public ITS market (Roadway/bus + Railway) outside the US with primary fleet, network and operator counts. Every number below was read from a source document fetched during this run (URL given); confidence flags follow the brief (high = primary or 2+ consistent sources; medium = single secondary; low = inferred/unclear scope).

**Research method / caveats.** WebFetch was network-blocked (ENOTFOUND on every host) and the shared WebSearch budget was exhausted after ~43 queries, but `curl` through the session proxy reached most primary hosts, so the core figures come from the actual UITP, ACEA, DfT, ORR, VDV, UTPF, MoRTH, PIB, China MOT, Eurostat/EC Pocketbook, BRTData and BIC documents (converted with pdftotext). A handful of hosts refused the proxy (xxgk.mot.gov.cn 2024 bulletin, CAMET, tfl.gov.uk, cutaactu.ca, mowasalat.com, rta.ae, IEA); those cells are marked medium and rely on search snippets that were cross-checked arithmetically where possible. Date of research: 2026-11-07.

---

## 1. Key takeaways

1. **Urban rail is a ~200,000-vehicle, ~36,000 km installed base and it is growing fast outside Europe/North America.** UITP's Global Metro Figures 2024 (data at 31 Dec 2023) count **202 urban agglomerations, 831 lines, 20,453 km, 14,865 stations, 158,871 metro cars and 58.0 bn annual trips**; cars grew +15% and km +18% in only three years (2020-23), and +61% in cars over 2013-23. Light rail/tram adds **403 cities, ~15,800 km and ~37,000 vehicles** (UITP, end-2021), trolleybuses **257 cities / 22,137 vehicles** (UITP, Dec 2024).
2. **Asia-Pacific is 58% of the metro fleet (91,920 cars) and 63% of km (12,957 km); Europe is 16% of cars (25,325) and 14% of km (2,834).** China alone is 43% of global metro km (8,788 km in 2023 per UITP; 10,326 km of metro and 11,651 km of all urban rail at end-2025 per MOT) and holds **76,900 urban-rail vehicles (end-2025, MOT)**, i.e. roughly half the world's metro cars. Over 800 km of new metro per year opened globally since 2010, two-thirds in mainland China.
3. **The addressable non-US transit bus base is roughly 1.3-1.6 million vehicles, of which China is ~630,000 (87.5% new-energy) and the EU+UK ~850,000 registered buses & coaches (of which roughly 35-45% are urban/PSO transit buses, e.g. Germany 35,401 VDV buses vs 85,559 registered; France 17,864 UTPF buses vs 94,542; GB ~34,600 local buses vs 125,650).** India adds ~147,000 STU buses (only ~65,000 organised city buses), Japan 57,914 route buses, Australia ~41,900 commercial-use buses, Canada ~16,100 transit buses.
4. **Fleet renewal and electrification are the demand triggers for on-vehicle ITS.** EU bus registrations hit 38,238 in 2025 (+7.5%; 23.8% electrically chargeable, 60% of city-bus registrations zero-emission), the EU fleet averages 12.2 years with only 3.1-3.2% battery-electric, UK local buses are 12% zero-emission, Europe registered 11,607 e-buses in 2025 (+48%), India's CSE study says 5.8 lakh buses reach end-of-life FY2026-30 and urban procurement must rise from ~2,500 to ~41,500/yr, and China is in a replacement cycle (fleet shrinking 2-4%/yr while NEV share rises to 87.5%).
5. **Rail rolling stock outside the US is dominated by Europe and China.** EU-27 holds 96,895 coaches/railcar trailers plus 63,604 locomotives & railcars (2023); GB 15,348 passenger vehicles (Mar 2025, 24 operators, 16.7-yr average age); Germany's VDV members 20,857 passenger-rail vehicles; China Railway 83,000 passenger coaches incl. 41,836 EMU cars (5,230 standard trainsets, end-2025); India ~1,095-1,128 km of metro in 26 cities with ~1,000 km under construction and >2,000 metro coaches built domestically.
6. **Operator fragmentation differs sharply by geography, which shapes go-to-market:** Japan has 2,337 route-bus operators (96% SMEs), Germany ~700 VDV members, France ~158 UTPF urban networks, GB hundreds of bus operators (Ticketer alone serves ~220 = ~50% of market) but only 24 rail operators, India 69 SRTUs (58 reporting), Canada 102 urban transit agencies, Seoul 64 bus companies under one authority, while Gulf metros (Riyadh, Dubai, Doha) are single-authority greenfield systems with international O&M contractors (RATP Dev/SAPTCO, Keolis-MHI, RKH Qitarat).
7. **Market-size cross-checks:** UNIFE/Roland Berger put the global rail-supply market at EUR 201.8 bn p.a. (2021-23) growing 3.0% real to EUR 240.8 bn (2027-29); SCI Verkehr sizes new rolling stock alone at EUR 64.6 bn (2024) with ~50,000 units produced p.a.; S&P/ACEA count 389,141 buses (>6 t) built worldwide in 2025 (China 118,416; India 112,757; EU 28,266).
