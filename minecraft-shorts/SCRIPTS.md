# Six one-minute stories: scripts for review

Each script is what the narrator says, word for word. The words also appear on screen one at a time, timed to the voice.
Every script is 161 to 165 words, which is about 50 seconds of speech and a film of about one minute.
The shot-by-shot plan for each film is in `SHOTLISTS.md`.

Production order is the order below.

## 1. The Hello

*Told by a baby villager. 161 words.*

**Your idea:** In this town the player used to protect us. But one day he went into the woods wearing his best armor and now he just stands around and watches us. (Player got replaced by herobrine) (This one is told from the perspective of a baby villager)

**The story:** A baby villager who only wants back the crouch-crouch hello the player gave him every morning watches the thing that came out of the woods stand one house closer each day, walks up to it after a night in which it let a zombie knock until sunrise, and gets his hello from a face that does not have the player's eyes.

**Narration:**

> In this town, the player used to protect us. He fought every zombie that knocked on our door. When he slept, the night just ended. Every morning he crouched at me, twice. That meant hello. One day he put on his best armor and walked into the woods. He came back at sunrise without it. He didn't crouch. Now he just stands around and watches us. The grownups say players stand still sometimes. I watch him back. He used to take the trunk and leave the tree floating. Now he only takes the leaves. Nobody built the sand pyramid in the meadow. Every time I look, he is one house closer. He doesn't sleep anymore. The nights last all night. Last night a zombie knocked on our door until sunrise. He stood beside it and watched. So this morning I walked up to him and waited for my hello. He crouched, once, and he stayed down. The player had blue eyes.

## 2. Two Apples

*Told by a baby villager. 164 words.*

**Your idea:** In this town the player has an underground base. My dad got taken down there. It's a villager trading hall where they turn villagers into zombies then cure for better trades. (This one is told from the perspective of a baby villager)

**The story:** A baby villager goes down into the player's underground trading hall to find his dad and calmly tells us how the hall's one zombie turns dad green so the player can cure him into a one-emerald librarian, until the last line shows that the zombie the player keeps is the child himself.

**Narration:**

> In this town, the player has an underground base. My dad got taken down there. The player walked into him until he was downstairs. That evening, I jumped on dad's bed. Nobody told me to stop. So I went down to find him. It's a trading hall, and dad has the last tiny room. The player keeps one zombie down here. He pulls a lever, and dad's wall opens. The zombie runs in and hugs him. On Hard, we always turn. Dad goes green under his hat. The player throws a potion and feeds him a golden apple. He only ever brings one. I always hope he'll bring two. Dad shakes for five minutes. Then he's dad again, and Mending costs one emerald. Dad thinks the player saved him. He never looks behind him. Babies grow up in twenty minutes. I'm still small. The night I came down, the zombie found me first. The player only needs one zombie. He kept the smaller one.

## 3. Twenty Minutes

*Told by a baby villager. 161 words.*

**Your idea:** In this village everyone is afraid of the Minecraft MC for making farms out of the iron golems. Villagers get trapped at the top blah blah blah (This one is told from the perspective of a baby villager)

**The story:** A baby villager, the only one in the village who is not afraid of the player, climbs the dirt staircase he forgot to take down and slips through a one-block gap into his iron farm to be with its mom. Its twenty-minute childhood runs out inside, it no longer fits through the gap, and it becomes the fourth frightened villager making his iron.

**Narration:**

> In this village, everyone is afraid of the player. I'm not. He only takes grownups. My mom lives at the top of his glass tower now. I would wave, but our arms don't do that. He gave her a bed, and a zombie in a box. The zombie can't reach her. It doesn't have to. When we get scared, an iron golem comes to save us. It comes every time. The water pushes it into the lava. He takes the iron and leaves the poppies. He never took down his dirt staircase. At the top there's a gap, one block high. Grownups don't fit, but I do. Mom never looks at me, only at the zombie. Childhood here lasts twenty minutes. I spent mine with her. I was still inside when it ended. Now I don't fit. He counted us twice, then brought one more bed. I'm still not afraid of the player. I'm afraid of the zombie. Apparently that counts.

## 4. The Crammys

*Told by a player in a town of players. 163 words.*

**Your idea:** In this town everyone is musical except me. The biggest Crammy party is today and I'm the only one who never has anything to perform (this one is in a village of all players)

**The story:** The one player in a musical town with nothing to perform at the Crammys walks out past the last torch and gets himself shot so a skeleton will kill a creeper for a music disc. It is the broken disc eleven, the sound of someone running away, and it sounds so much like him being chased home every night that he wins Best Vocals.

**Narration:**

> In this town, everyone is musical except me. Zuri keeps her note block on wool, so she plays guitar. Kai keeps his on gold, so he plays bells. Sunny keeps his on a pumpkin, and we don't talk about it. Tonight was the Crammys, our biggest party of the year. Everyone performs, and I never have anything. I just get chased home every night. But a creeper drops a music disc if a skeleton kills it. So I walked out past the last torch. I found a creeper. I found a skeleton. I stood in the middle. The skeleton shot me. Then it shot me again. Then it shot the creeper. A disc dropped. I ran to the stage with arrows still in me. Nobody asked. I put my disc in the jukebox. It was disc eleven, the broken one. It's just someone running away and breathing hard. Nobody moved. Kai said it sounded just like me coming home. I won Best Vocals.

## 5. Leg Day

*Told by a player in a town of players. 165 words.*

**Your idea:** In this town everyone wants to get ripped. there's a chicken farm. Cow farm for protein shakes, but I'm a redstoner. Then one day one of his traps saves the village (this one is in a village of all players)

**The story:** In a town of gym-mad players who carry twelve anvils up a wall every day, the one thin-armed redstoner quietly puts a sticky piston under each anvil. When a pillager patrol shrugs off their fists, one lever drops the whole rack on the road, and the town, still calling redstone cheating, proudly hauls the weights back up without knowing their workout is how his trap reloads.

**Narration:**

> In this town, everyone wants to get ripped. Steve mines stone with his fists. It drops nothing, and he counts that as one rep. There's a chicken farm, and a cow farm for protein shakes. Milk doesn't even fill you up. I haven't told them. But I'm a redstoner. My arms are three pixels wide, and theirs are four. Steve asked how much my piston lifts, and I said twelve blocks. He said that's cheating. Every day they carry twelve anvils up the wall. They call it leg day. Under each anvil, I put a sticky piston. Nobody asked why. Then the pillagers came. Everyone ran out to punch them. Pillagers have crossbows, so everyone hid behind the cows. I flipped one lever. Twelve pistons pulled back, and twelve anvils fell on the road. That left a banner and a bottle. Steve still says redstone is cheating. But they carried all twelve back up before dark. They call it leg day. I call it reloading.

## 6. Six Seconds

*Told by a piglin brute. 164 words.*

**Your idea:** In this bastion I'm the only one who doesn't like gold. (This is told from the perspective of a piglin) this one is about speedrunners and how they trap piglins in holes to trade with them but one piglin just keeps attacking the player and doesn't care about gold. He keeps seeing the player in different worlds and messing up his speed run

**The story:** A piglin brute who guards his bastion and sees no point in gold ends the same gold-booted speedrunner's run in world after world, starts waiting for him at the gate, and is left behind when the runner moves to an older version of the world from before brutes were added, staring at an ingot for six seconds and feeling nothing.

**Narration:**

> In this bastion, I'm the only one who doesn't like gold. My brothers will stare at one ingot for six seconds. Somebody has to guard the place. One day, a player ran in. He wore golden boots, so my brothers thought he was family. He dug a hole, and they fell in. He threw gold, and they threw back ender pearls. Mostly gravel. I hit him with my axe. He turned into smoke, and the whole world ended. New world, different bastion, same boots. Every world has a bastion, and every bastion has me. He hid on a pillar and threw gold at me. I looked at it for zero seconds. I can wait, and he can't. I ended a full stack of worlds. I started waiting at the gate. One day, he didn't come. He found an older version of the world, from before they added me. In that bastion, everyone likes gold. So today I'm staring at an ingot. Six seconds. Nothing.
