| 8 | The player keeps one zombie down here. | The back corner of the hall, camera on the floor, focus on Steve's boots as his legs walk past. | 9 | He pulls a lever, and dad's wall opens. | At the front of dad's cell. Close on Steve's hand flipping the lever on the stone pier. | 10 | The zombie runs in and hugs him. | From the aisle, low and a little to one side, the lectern filling the bottom third. The attacker is not in the picture at all: beside his robe there is only the black doorway. Dad's eyes stay front. |
| 11 | On Hard, we always turn. | Tight three-quarter portrait of dad: big nose, brow, book hat. A second blow: the red flash again, a small jolt. His colours come back. He is still looking straight ahead. He does not fall and there is no smoke. |
| 12 | Dad goes green under his hat. | Frontal, symmetrical, locked off on the whole cell. One more jolt, and on that frame dad is simply replaced where he stands, exactly as in the game: a zombie villager in the very same book hat and robe, green face, arms out over the lectern, finishing the little hop. No particles. The black doorway is still open behind him. Hold on the hat. |
| 13 | The player throws a potion and feeds him a golden apple. | Side-on along the counter. | 14 | He only ever brings one. | Close on the single golden apple in Steve's hand, torchlit, the brightest thing in the frame. The green face is soft behind it. |

| 18 | Dad thinks the player saved him. | Portrait of dad. His head follows Steve walking away up the aisle with the book until it reaches its stop, and stays turned that way. The book's glint goes soft in the distance. |
| 19 | He never looks behind him. | Same cell from the side and lower, through the end wall, so the wall behind him is in the picture. Dad still gazes up the aisle. The camera slides slowly off his face onto the stone directly behind his back, the seam of the piston wall, and holds there. |
| 20 | Babies grow up in twenty minutes. | The square at the same dusk. Two baby villagers chase each other round the well, staggered. | 23 | The player only needs one zombie. | Same spot. Steve with an iron sword: one swing, the last blow.
